# Velero Roadmap

Velero maintains its roadmap in the project wiki. The wiki holds the current
roadmap, archived roadmaps from previous release cycles, and guidance on how
roadmap items are proposed and prioritized.

- [Velero Wiki roadmap](https://github.com/velero-io/velero/wiki/)

## How the roadmap is maintained

Roadmap items are driven by the community and the maintainers:

- Larger features start as a design proposal in the [`design/`](design/)
  directory, following [`design/_template.md`](design/_template.md). Proposals
  are discussed on the pull request and in the community meetings before they
  are accepted.
- Feature requests and bugs are tracked as
  [GitHub issues](https://github.com/velero-io/velero/issues).
- Priorities are reviewed by the maintainers each release cycle and discussed in
  the bi-weekly [community meetings](https://velero.io/community/), which are
  open to everyone.

Anyone can propose a roadmap item by opening an issue or a design proposal, or by
raising it in a community meeting.
